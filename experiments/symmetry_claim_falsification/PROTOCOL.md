# Frozen symmetry-only falsification protocol

Frozen 2026-08-28 before inspecting any new control result.

## Targeted claim

The previous cycle retained an ansatz/event-specific generic comparison-rank
signature on symmetry-rich MIS instances.  Its weakest control used phases
that broke graph symmetry.  This protocol asks whether the entire surviving
rank deficit is instead generic in the automorphism-invariant Hilbert subspace.

## Frozen cases and cuts

Use every previously frozen development and held-out row: triangle chains of
4 and 5 and the triangle ring of 6, each in natural and spectral order.  Test
exactly the cuts previously labelled `deficit_cuts`; do not select new cuts
after seeing the controls.

## Controls

For each row, build the exact orbit partition generated by every within-triangle
leaf swap plus chain reflection or ring rotation/reflection.

1. **QAOA replay:** evolve the archived three schedules in the full graph-orbit
   quotient and require their dense comparison ranks to reproduce the archived
   profile exactly.
2. **Full-automorphism Haar:** draw two independent normalized complex Gaussian
   vectors in the normalized full graph-orbit basis, for five deterministic
   seeds.
3. **Orbit-phase scramble:** preserve the magnitudes of archived QAOA quotient
   coefficients but assign independent phases to every full graph orbit in the
   two states, for five deterministic seeds.
4. **Twin-only Haar:** draw generic states in the larger twin-count quotient,
   without enforcing chain reflection or ring dihedral symmetry, for five
   deterministic seeds.

All controls preserve state normalization.  The first three preserve the full
graph automorphism group.  The fourth isolates how much of the rank is explained
by the local twin backend alone.  The existing unrestricted Haar result remains
the symmetry-breaking reference.

## Decision rule

- **Kill ansatz-specific rank claim:** on every frozen deficit cut, all five
  full-automorphism Haar pairs and all five orbit-phase pairs have the same rank
  as the archived QAOA pair.
- **Narrow to full graph symmetry:** full-automorphism controls match QAOA but
  twin-only Haar has a larger rank on at least one cut.
- **Residual ansatz structure survives:** a full-automorphism control has rank
  strictly larger than QAOA on any frozen deficit cut, reproducibly across all
  five seeds.
- Any seed-dependent rank is a numerical/algebraic warning and blocks a claim
  until tolerance scaling is resolved.

Numerical rank uses the already frozen absolute threshold `1e-12`.  Exact rank
profiles, seeds, orbit counts, protocol hash, and replay checks must be written
atomically before interpretation.
