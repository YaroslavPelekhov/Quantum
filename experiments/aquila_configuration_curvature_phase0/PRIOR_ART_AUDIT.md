# Prior-art boundary

The broad novelty claim is killed before validation.

- Greschner et al., PRL 2014, derive density-dependent complex hopping from a
  temporal/Fourier response and distinguish gaugeable from non-gaugeable
  Peierls phases: https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.3150
- Mumford, PRA 2022, creates interaction-induced plaquette flux on a many-body
  Fock-state lattice and studies the Hofstadter spectrum, Chern marker, and
  chiral edge motion: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00742
- Lienhard et al., PRX 2020, experimentally demonstrate occupancy-dependent
  complex hopping, chiral motion, and an anyonic phase in a Rydberg system:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10357
- Nishad et al., PRA 2023, Floquet-engineer Rydberg Peierls phases and chiral/DM
  exchange using local Stark phases: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07041
- Koeylueoglu et al., PRL 2025, use blockade-regime ground-Rydberg detuning
  micromotion and explicitly identify site-resolved detuning as a route to
  chiral interactions: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02741
- Matwiejew et al., 2026, implement a real-edge continuous-time quantum walk on
  the independent-set configuration graph using Aquila hardware:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00386
- Goerg et al., Nature Physics 2019, directly perform Peierls-phase tomography:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05895

A targeted search did not find the exact conjunction "Aquila AHS + one fixed
grayscale mask + native C6 tails + independent-set Wilson flux".  Combining
known ingredients on a new platform is not enough for an A-star theory claim.

The only credible stronger direction would be a complete one-mask curvature
compiler: necessary-and-sufficient flatness, dimension/rank of the attainable
plaquette-flux tensor, controlled Magnus error, and tight time/action/leakage/
noise lower and constructive upper bounds, followed by phase-sensitive hardware
validation on scalable held-out graph families.  A single two-atom square does
not meet that standard.
