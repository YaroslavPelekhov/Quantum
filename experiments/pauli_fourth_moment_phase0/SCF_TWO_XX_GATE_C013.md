# C013: two interacting published XX atoms, not another single-atom path

Registered 2026-09-08 before calculation. Do not start this discovery until
C012 has complete independent acceptance, including its negative control.

Freeze ONE 24-vertex graph as follows. Take two disjoint copies of the full
source XX graph, each with original vertices 7 and 8 removed. In each copy
let A={1,2,13} and B={4,5,13}, retaining original labels within each copy.
Make all pairs between B_left and B_right edges. Add u,v and the edge uv;
join u to every vertex of A_left and v to every vertex of A_right.
No other new edges. Lexicographic order is left original labels, then
right original labels, then u,v. Verify claw-freeness and the proposed
simplicial clique {u,v}; failure rejects the target, not the definition.

The question is whether positive facets connecting BOTH atoms remain
outside rank, alpha-two, order-nine, and induced C009/C011 routes. A second
copy creates a simultaneous-boundary problem that a single atom plus a
path does not test. This graph construction and classical strip machinery
are published concepts; no graph-discovery or polyhedral novelty claim.

Enumerate stable sets recursively with exact bit masks, discover facets
with cdd.gmp and check its rational H/V roundtrip. One process capped at
300 seconds; stop if more than 10000 stable sets or 10000 facets occur.
No Pauli/beta optimization, randomness, QPU, paid jobs or new graph search.
Route in the order above. Select the first lexicographically sorted
uncovered facet with maximum support size, if any. Independently check
its validity on ALL enumerated stable sets and its exact affine rank.
Neither a cdd roundtrip nor one independently checked facet establishes
independent completeness of this 24-dimensional hull. Do NOT extend the
15-dimensional cube-verifier cap for this gate.

If no row is uncovered, this is a negative novelty screen, not a new
quantum theorem. If a row is uncovered, it is a precise quantum proof
obligation, not a physical violation or confirmed novelty. Before operator
experiments it requires another registration specifying signs, sector
relations, exact identity and falsification controls.

Prior-art guard: de Vries, Discrete Tomography, Packing and Covering and
Stable Set Problems, pp.150-151, Proposition 5.9.4 and Lemma 5.9.5,
review classical double-edge subdivision (Wolsey 1976). They preserve a
given valid/facet row under their conditions; they do not by themselves
give every facet or a quantum composition theorem. Arbitrarily lengthening
C012's path is therefore not selected as an independent novelty claim.
