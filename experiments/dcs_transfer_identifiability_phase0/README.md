# DCS fidelity-transfer identifiability: phase 0

This phase asks a narrow question: can classically verifiable Clifford
companions give a nonperturbative lower bound on the post-selected fidelity of
a classically hard Clifford+T circuit under a common, arbitrarily correlated
stochastic Pauli fault-path distribution?

The experiment starts from two exact negative controls: a counterexample to an
asymptotic harmless-fault rarity claim and a two-qubit witness showing that
reference fidelity plus syndrome data alone do not identify target fidelity.
The confirmatory work then tests a stronger randomized-companion certificate.

Read `PREREGISTRATION.md` before interpreting any result.  The branch is not an
A-star claim unless one of its two predeclared theorem outcomes passes every
gate.
