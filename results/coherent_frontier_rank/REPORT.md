# Coherent frontier-rank controls

## Verdict

The aves deficit is reproducible and belongs to the ansatz/event geometry, not
to one lucky parameter pair.

- Independent probability-preserving phase scrambling saturates the generic
  event-matching cap on **100% of 40 eligible cuts**.
- A common product of single-qubit phase gates preserves every original rank,
  as required by local-unitary covariance.
- Published-LR vs matched-random, published-LR vs evolutionary, and
  matched-random vs evolutionary have **identical rank profiles** for both
  orderings.
- Replacing both states by their entrywise magnitudes also preserves every rank.
- All three pairs meet the 25% deficit gate on the same 29 ordering/cut rows.

Thus the deficit is not explained by event support, diagonal probabilities,
one parameter schedule, or the full-state Schmidt rank.  Arbitrary nonlocal
diagonal phases destroy it; qubit-local phases do not.
